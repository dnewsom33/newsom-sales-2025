Our Story

Dalton Newsom and Brittany Shale crossed paths at a mutual friend’s summer barbecue on a sun‑drenched afternoon. What began as a conversation about travel and music quickly blossomed into a friendship and, before long, an inseparable partnership. Over the years they have explored new cities together, cooked elaborate dinners for friends and family, and weathered life’s ups and downs side by side. Dalton’s wit and Brittany’s warmth make every day feel like an adventure, and together they are stronger than ever. They are thrilled to gather their loved ones in Toronto to celebrate the next chapter of their journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the timeline for the day?

The ceremony will begin promptly at 5:00 PM, followed by a cocktail hour, dinner at 7:00 PM and dancing into the night. Please arrive early so we can begin on time.

What should I wear?

Formal attire is requested. For inspiration, think black‑tie optional – suits and cocktail dresses are perfectly appropriate.

Is parking available?

Yes. Casa Loma has on‑site parking for a flat rate of about $20 per vehicle (subject to change). Additional parking is available nearby at George Brown College. Rideshare services may drop guests off free of charge in the east lot.

Will there be shuttle transportation?

At this time we are not planning a shuttle service; however, the venue is easily accessible via TTC (Dupont or Spadina station) and rideshares. If transportation plans change we will update this page closer to the event.

How do I RSVP?

Invitations will include an RSVP card and a link to our digital response form. Please respond by the indicated date so we can plan accordingly. If you have any dietary restrictions, be sure to let us know.
